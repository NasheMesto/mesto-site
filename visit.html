Visit MESTO

MESTO is an artist-run restaurant, bar, and cultural space near Thong Sala on Koh Phangan. Come for dinner and cocktails, discover our tropical garden with swings and a hand-woven rope park, meet people from the island’s creative community, or stay for one of our free events—from live music and open mics to exhibitions, workshops, cinema nights, talks, and queer balls. You can also read more about MESTO and how the space came to life.

How to Find Us

MESTO is located in Don Sai, about one minute from the Makro store in Thong Sala. The easiest way to find us is to follow our location on Google Maps. You can also use the interactive map below.

Opening Hours

MESTO is open every day from 4:00 PM to 11:30 PM.

What’s Happening at MESTO

Our event schedule changes throughout the week. Follow @nashemesto.kpg on Instagram for upcoming concerts, workshops, exhibitions, parties, cinema nights, and other events.
